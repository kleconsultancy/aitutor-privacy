Account and Data Deletion

AI Tutor – Smart Learning

Parents or guardians can request deletion of their AI Tutor – Smart Learning account and associated child profile information.

How to request deletion

Email excelvba.studio@gmail.com with the subject AI Tutor Account Deletion. State the email address associated with the parent account and whether you want to delete the entire parent account or a specific child profile. Do not send passwords or child PINs.

We may ask you to verify ownership of the parent account before acting on a request. You do not need to reinstall the app to contact us.

Data covered by the request

Requests may cover the parent account record, associated child profiles, and learning progress or practice history linked to the account, subject to technical limitations and applicable retention obligations. Data stored only on a device may need to be removed from that device separately.

Retention and confirmation

We will respond to explain the deletion process and confirm completion or identify any information that must be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, or security reasons. Please contact us if you need assistance with an in-app deletion option.
