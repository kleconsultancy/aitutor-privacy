Account and Data Deletion

AI Tutor – Smart Learning
Updated: 30 October 2026

Parents or guardians can request deletion of their AI Tutor – Smart Learning parent account and associated child data.

Request inside the app

  1. Sign in to the parent account and open Parent Area.
  2. Choose Account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm the current parent password and type DELETE.
  4. Wait for server confirmation. The app shows whether confirmation or local cleanup is still pending and offers retry if it fails.

When the request is confirmed, the app clears that account’s local child profiles, learning records, PINs, settings, AI access and usage records, and temporary camera cache, then signs out. Recovery can resume after the app is reopened. A request being accepted is not confirmation that cloud deletion is complete.

Request without the app

Email excelvba.studio@gmail.com with the subject AI Tutor Account Deletion. Include the parent account email address and state that you want the account and its associated data deleted. You do not need to reinstall the app. Do not send passwords, PINs or unnecessary child details. We may verify ownership before acting.

What is deleted

The support team operates a separate deletion process that removes the parent record and associated child profiles, synced practice attempts, mastery, wrong-answer and daily-practice summaries, trial/account records and any account-owned licence or usage records under that parent, then deletes the Firebase Authentication account. The process checks its results and can be retried after interruption. No fixed completion deadline is promised here; contact support for status and confirmation.

What may remain

A minimal security marker containing the old Firebase user identifier and deletion timestamp remains to block recreation using that identifier. No automatic expiry is currently configured for this marker. Support correspondence may remain to handle and document the request. This deletion process does not itself erase provider operational logs or copies on other devices; contact support for the scope of retained records and the reason for retention.

Other devices may need separate local cleanup or app storage removal. App cache cleanup does not delete original images in your device gallery. Legacy temporary photos without owner information are cleared as shared cache; newer child-scoped cache folders are kept separate. Uninstalling the app or clearing device storage alone does not submit a cloud account deletion request.
