Privacy Policy

AI Tutor – Smart Learning
Effective date: 27 October 2026

AI Tutor – Smart Learning is an educational app designed for children to practice learning activities under the management of a parent or guardian. This policy explains how information is handled when using the app.

Information we process

Depending on the features used, the app processes parent account details such as an email address and authentication identifiers; child profiles such as a nickname and school grade; and learning activity information such as practice answers, progress and review history. The app may also process technical information required for authentication, security, and service operation.

How information is used

Information is used to provide parent sign-in, manage child profiles, deliver educational practice, display progress, protect accounts and maintain the service. We do not use child profiles to display targeted advertising.

Service providers

The app uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, for account authentication and application data. These services may process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy documentation. Other SDKs and data flows, if present in the published app, must be reflected in this policy before publication.

Children and parent controls

Parent or guardian accounts manage child profiles. Children should not provide unnecessary personal details in profile fields. Purchase and subscription management is not presented to children in the current free release.

Storage, security and retention

We use access controls and service security features intended to restrict access to account information. No security method is guaranteed to be perfect. Information is retained while needed to provide the service, subject to deletion requests and applicable legal requirements. Some data may be stored locally on the device and may be lost when the app is removed.

Account and data deletion

Parents or guardians can request deletion of an account and associated data by emailing excelvba.studio@gmail.com. See our Account and Data Deletion page for instructions. We may ask for verification of account ownership before processing a request. We will explain any data that must be retained for legal or security reasons.

Policy updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date.

Contact

Privacy inquiries: excelvba.studio@gmail.com.
