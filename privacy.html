Privacy Policy

AI Tutor – Smart Learning
Updated: 30 October 2026

AI Tutor – Smart Learning supports children’s learning under a parent or guardian’s management.

Parent accounts and child profiles

We process the parent email address, authentication identifier and account security information through Firebase Authentication. Child profiles contain a name or nickname, school grade, avatar text and, where applicable, mathematics subject level. Please avoid entering unnecessary personal details. The Firebase password is not stored in the local learning database. Parent and Child PINs are stored as salted hashes on the device.

Learning records and cloud sync

Practice answers and attempts, current mastery, wrong-answer summaries and daily-practice summaries are stored on the device and synced with Cloud Firestore under the parent account. Other learning records, including competition attempts and mastery history, are stored locally. Signing out alone does not erase local learning records; account ownership filtering separates parents on the same device.

Photos and AI features

Photo-based AI guidance and purchase functions are not available in the current public free release. Developer test builds can send a selected question image, grade, question text and follow-up answers to Gemini or Groq when a developer enables that feature. Temporary camera copies are stored in the app cache. Deleting an account does not delete originals in the device gallery. Any future public AI feature must be reflected in this notice before it is enabled.

Services, purposes and security

Firebase Authentication provides parent sign-in and account management. Cloud Firestore stores synced profiles and learning records. Firebase App Check with Play Integrity supports app security and may process technical and integrity information; authentication also processes technical connection information. We use account and learning data to provide, secure and maintain learning functions and progress displays. We do not use child profiles or learning records for targeted advertising. Network service connections use HTTPS; no security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Request deletion inside the app

Open Parent Area, choose Account, then Delete account. Confirm the parent password and type DELETE. The app records a deletion request. It distinguishes a request awaiting server confirmation from an accepted request and pauses learning for that account while recovery is pending. After confirmation, it clears that account’s local child profiles, learning records, PINs, settings, AI access and usage records, and temporary camera cache, then signs out. Interrupted local cleanup can be retried after reopening the app.

Cloud deletion and requests outside the app

Cloud deletion is completed by the support team using a separately operated, verified deletion process; accepting a request does not mean cloud deletion is already complete. The process removes the parent record and its associated child profiles, synced learning records and account-related records, then removes the Firebase Authentication account. You can also email excelvba.studio@gmail.com from the parent account email address to request deletion without reinstalling the app. Do not send passwords or PINs. We may verify account ownership. No fixed completion deadline is promised here; contact support for the status and confirmation of your request.

Retained records and deletion limits

A minimal deletion marker containing the old Firebase user identifier and deletion timestamp is retained to prevent that identifier from recreating account data. No automatic expiry is currently configured for this security marker. The account deletion process does not itself erase support correspondence, provider operational logs or copies on other devices. Support correspondence may be retained to handle and document your request; ask support what remains and why. Other devices may require separate local cleanup or app storage removal. Legacy temporary camera files without owner information are cleared as shared cache; newer captures are separated by child.

Parent controls, updates and contact

A parent or guardian manages child profiles and access to parent controls. Child screens do not present purchase or upgrade prompts. You can ask questions about access, correction, deletion or retained data at excelvba.studio@gmail.com. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in an updated notice. The public Privacy Policy is at https://kleconsultancy.github.io/aitutor-privacy/privacy.html and deletion instructions are at https://kleconsultancy.github.io/aitutor-privacy/delete-account.html.
