AI Tutor – Smart Learning supports children’s learning under a parent or guardian’s management.

Parent account and learning space

Only parents register and sign in. Each parent account has one default learning space. New child names, birthdays, avatars and actual school grades are not requested. Practice levels are freely chosen content preferences, not a claim about a child’s age or school placement. Parent email, authentication identifier and account security information support sign-in through Firebase Authentication. The Parent PIN is stored as a salted hash on the device; there is no separate child login or new Child PIN. Internal record identifiers are kept to preserve learning history and do not make the records anonymous.

Parent electronic consent — Singapore launch

Before new learning and uploads, a parent or authorised guardian reads the learning data notice and actively confirms authority and consent in the app. Both statements start unselected. For this Singapore launch, no individual support approval, signature scan or identity-document upload is part of the consent flow. Email verification confirms control of the mailbox, not guardianship. The server records the notice version, parent account identifier, declaration, consent method, generation, reference and timestamp, with a separate immutable withdrawal event if consent is withdrawn. Consent takes effect only after server confirmation. Parents can withdraw in Parent Area → Account → Manage learning data consent. Existing history and account deletion remain available. Giving consent again creates a new record. These account-linked consent events are included in account deletion; withdrawal alone does not delete them.

Learning records and cloud sync

Practice questions, answers and attempts, current mastery, wrong-answer summaries and daily-practice summaries are stored locally and supported records sync with Cloud Firestore under the parent account. Exercise levels may be apparent from question codes and practice records even though no actual school grade is requested. Competition attempts and mastery history remain local learning records. Signing out alone does not erase records; ownership filtering separates parents.

Photos and AI features

Photo-based AI guidance and purchase functions are not available in the current public free release. Developer test builds can send a selected question image, grade, question text and follow-up answers to Gemini or Groq when a developer enables that feature. Temporary camera copies are stored in the app cache. Deleting an account does not delete originals in the device gallery. Any future public AI feature must be reflected in this notice before it is enabled.

Services, purposes and security

Firebase Authentication supports parent sign-in and account management, including security-related IP and technical information. Cloud Firestore stores the account, learning space, consent events and supported learning records. Firebase installation identifiers and App Check with Play Integrity support app and backend security. We use these services for account access, learning, progress, personalised practice and protection against abuse. We do not use learning records for targeted advertising. Service connections use encryption in transit; no security method guarantees absolute protection.

Request deletion inside the app

Open Parent Area, Account, Delete account. Confirm the parent password and type DELETE. The app distinguishes a request awaiting server confirmation from an accepted request and pauses learning while recovery is pending. After confirmation it clears the account’s learning space, learning data, Parent PIN, workspace settings, AI access/usage and temporary camera cache, then signs out. Cloud deletion runs separately. Shared Firestore disk cache is also cleared; other accounts’ pending writes are preserved and may block completion until they sync. Their local learning records and cloud data are retained, though cache copies may be downloaded again. Interrupted cleanup can be retried after reopening.

Cloud deletion and requests outside the app

Cloud deletion is completed by the support team using a separately operated, verified deletion process; accepting a request does not mean cloud deletion is already complete. The process removes the parent record and its associated child profiles, synced learning records and account-related records, then removes the Firebase Authentication account. You can also email excelvba.studio@gmail.com from the parent account email address to request deletion without reinstalling the app. Do not send passwords or PINs. We may verify account ownership. No fixed completion deadline is promised here; contact support for the status and confirmation of your request.

Retained records and deletion limits

A minimal deletion marker containing the old Firebase user identifier and deletion timestamp is retained to prevent that identifier from recreating account data. No automatic expiry is currently configured for this security marker. The account deletion process does not itself erase support correspondence, provider operational logs or copies on other devices. Support correspondence may be retained to handle and document your request; ask support what remains and why. Other devices may require separate local cleanup or app storage removal. Legacy temporary camera files without owner information are cleared as shared cache; newer captures are separated by internal learning-record identifiers.

Parent controls, updates and contact

A parent or guardian manages the learning space, learning records and access to parent controls. Child screens do not present purchase or upgrade prompts. You can ask questions about access, correction, deletion or retained data at excelvba.studio@gmail.com. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in an updated notice. The public Privacy Policy is at https://kleconsultancy.github.io/aitutor-privacy/privacy.html and deletion instructions are at https://kleconsultancy.github.io/aitutor-privacy/delete-account.html.
